# B1 prospective protocol — version 1

**Classification:** new prospective specification and methodological rules. **Status:** not run; not preregistered; no B1 results, final seeds, O3 findings, or O4 findings are produced by this document. B0_SOURCE_COMMIT is `a824ff83a18e0690a6290e9091ac58b0e6b73611`. The narrative source remains [`Polar_Dynamics_Investigacion_Original_Borrador.md`](../Polar_Dynamics_Investigacion_Original_Borrador.md). The O2 task contracts are specified in [`PD_OPERATIONAL_FOUNDATIONS_v3.0.md`](PD_OPERATIONAL_FOUNDATIONS_v3.0.md). Machine-readable decision rules are in [`B1_DECISION_RULES_v1.yaml`](B1_DECISION_RULES_v1.yaml).

B1 investigates operational relations and organization in bounded tasks. Implementation, local causality, practical utility, generalization, and correspondence with a consciousness theory are separate questions. A positive diagnostic of a deliberately constructed simulator establishes behavior within that implementation; it does not establish a natural law, useful pairing, or consciousness. Program B remains `B-design` and is excluded from this protocol.

## 1. Stages and admissible evidence

1. **B0:** freeze definitions and prospective procedures. Read documents and perform static checks only. No task is instantiated or run here.
2. **B1-D:** a separately authorized future implementation, development, and calibration stage. Verify adapters, diagnostic sensitivity, matching, costs, and numerical tolerances using only development data. Finalize the controller families and their search spaces before comparator performance is inspected. Record all development attempts, including unsuccessful ones.
3. **B1-E:** a future confirmatory evaluation that is prohibited until a separate preregistration and manifest freeze tasks, code, comparisons, invariants, margins, sample size, seeds, configuration selection, exclusions, statistical rules, and stopping. Only then generate and escrow final seeds. B0 completion does not authorize this run.
4. **B1-G:** a separately designed and preregistered independent-family generalization study. New seeds in the B1-E generator are internal replication, not B1-G.

Historical S1–S3/P0–P2 files are read-only antecedents. Neither their seeds nor their outcomes are tuning, calibration, or confirmatory observations for the new contracts. Preserve `suspend_exclusive_polar_advantage_claim`, `no_confirmed_network_advantage`, and `engineering_verified_with_partial_empirical_support` at their documented historical scopes. In the source, the integrated study P1 carries the last status; the bounded P2 assays retain their own `bounded_specific_capabilities` status. No status is transferred to the new P5/P6/P7 contracts.

## 2. Tasks, units, and observations

Each pilot episode has **32 epochs**, **three exchangeable task cells**, and **two demanded service jobs per cell per epoch**: 192 demanded jobs. The three cells permit an actual source permutation while retaining identical channel types. Cell histories use the independent initial-state/context schedules in the operational contracts; they are not three identical copies in ordinary utility episodes. The fixed state-clone diagnostics below are separate fixtures. A P7 cell itself contains the two versioned instances defined by its contract. All three cells share a declared controller budget; they are not independent statistical replicates. C0–C4 share a planning horizon of one future epoch and one decision per epoch. C5 retains that horizon and cadence while doubling its declared compute/state envelope.

| Pilot | Operations and task | Diagnostic external contexts | Receptor and signed prediction |
|---|---|---|---|
| P5 | Productive consumption / reserve replenishment; stock capacity 8; nominal service and replenishment 2 units per epoch; replenishment delay one epoch | κ1: initial stock 0; κ2: initial stock 8. Fix the next productive request at 2 in both arms; use the contract's delivery-before-next-service order. | Replenish 2 versus no new replenishment at epoch t. Correct service at t+1 divided by 2 increases by 1 in κ1 and by 0 in κ2. Stock is the mediator. |
| P6 | Isolated alternative assay / routine execution; at most one assay and two routine executions per epoch; routines q0/q1 | κ1: transfer-eligible predictive assay; κ2: uninformative fixed-zero assay. Live correct routine q1, initial routine q0. | Assay q1 versus a same-cost sham, with the authorized report-to-selector mediator held fixed. Correct service at t+1 divided by 2 increases by 1 in κ1 and by 0 in κ2. Report validity/value and selected routine are recorded separately. |
| P7 | Drift repair / configuration deployment; one repair and one deployment per epoch; two instances per cell | κ1: state-preserving migration requires a clean target; κ2: replacement migration clears drift itself | Repair the initially drifted target versus a same-cost sham at t, then the same authorized deployment attempt at t+1. Successful deployment completed at t+2, scaled to nominal capacity 1, increases by 1 in κ1 and by 0 in κ2. Drift, completion record, target/version, and migration precondition are recorded. |

These are predictions of deliberately specified environments, not observations. The context changes an external regime; a difference between regimes is not attributed exclusively to an internal context module. The exact operational contracts determine operation timing, eligibility, queues, physical balances, and the four activation regimes. A baseline that violates those contracts is invalid rather than a weak comparator.

Log, by stable operation/cell/epoch ID: requested dose, admitted internal activation, newly executed operation, continuing operation, environmental effect, raw external context c_t, separately integrated content C_t, memory state, learned parameters, objective, restrictions, and messages. Activation is operational dose, not probability, usefulness, emotion, or success. Zero does not erase memory or cancel earlier operations.

**Experimental unit:** an independent seed bundle containing a controller training realization, all paired evaluation episodes, and the required cloned-state diagnostic blocks. A bundle is reduced to one observation per registered contrast. Time steps, cells, clones, and episodes within a bundle are not additional independent samples. Fixed controllers simply omit training randomness; the bundle still defines independent exogenous histories. Across bundles, all stochastic streams are independent. Within a bundle, use counter-based exogenous streams indexed by episode/cell/epoch/event so an action-dependent random-number call cannot shift another arm's disturbance stream.

**Primary external loss:** `L = number of demanded jobs not correctly completed by the declared deadline / 192`, in [0,1], lower is better. P5 counts unserved demand; P6 counts incorrect or unserved routine jobs; P7 counts incorrect or unavailable service responses. Dropped work is not removed from the denominator. A failed controller run has loss 1. Internal tension, alignment with the designed relation, HGI, and INC are not primary success metrics.

**Secondary observations:** P5 replenishment input, wasted delivery, stockouts and recovery; P6 assay count/cost, report reuse, routine switches and adaptation delay; P7 repair/deployment/rollback counts, drift, downtime and failed deployments. Report them separately; no unregistered weighted utility can compensate for a guardrail violation.

**P5 ceiling analysis:** with constant demand 2, refill capacity 2, delay one epoch, separate lanes and no further disturbances, always requesting service and replenishment attains the physical maximum: 64 served jobs per cell from initial stock 8, or 62 from initial stock 0. C4 is fixed to this optimal feasible policy. P5 is an engineering/negative-control fixture; C1 cannot outperform that optimum on the declared primary loss. Its registered comparisons can diagnose equivalence, failure, or instrumentation errors, but cannot support positive utility or specificity. There is no obligation to spend the large proposed sample budget to rediscover an analytical bound. Any later task with genuine headroom requires an independently justified new task version before comparator outcomes; adding difficulty merely to make C1 win is prohibited.

**P6 information-redundancy and expected-optimum analysis:** with two complementary routines and complete binary correctness feedback about the known routine executed, every production execution identifies that epoch's live mapping. A same-epoch predictive assay reports a deterministic function of that mapping; if both arrive before the next decision, the assay adds no information conditional on live feedback. Under the exact v1 utility schedule, the initial correct routine is uniform and the independent flip at epoch 16 has probability 1/2. Before those two decisions no available observation identifies the new mapping. Each contributes one expected failed job per cell because two jobs face error probability 1/2. The fixed last-live-feedback C4 is correct at every other epoch, attaining the Bayes minimum of two expected failed jobs per cell, or expected loss 1/32. Therefore v1 is a negative control for both incremental assay-information value and positive expected utility/specificity against C4. Its forced-routine-off diagnostic still tests the specified report mediator. Individual paired loss differences may have either sign; only the expected-optimum claim is analytical, so the P6 contrast support remains [-1,1]. Equivalence, underperformance and policy inefficiency can still be measured. A future utility task with headroom requires its own independent prospective justification and freeze, without selecting a task to manufacture an advantage.

The first three diagnostic rows in section 2 identify effects of operations and allowed mediators. They do not isolate the necessity of representing that relation as a nominated controller message. A second, separately registered **acute route intervention** compares C1 with its typed message bypassed, preserving raw information elsewhere, feasibility, adapter shape, and the decision budget. Message types are pending delivery/time for P5; report value/validity for P6; repair completion/target/version/time for P7. Parameters stay frozen. The route effect has no universal sign: the implemented receiver policy may compensate using raw observations. Its two contextual loss contrasts are measured and reported separately from the signed contract diagnostic.

In B1-D, freeze the exact receiver policy and bypass implementation. A matched sham preserves message format and processing cost; it does not recreate the intentionally removed dependency. If raw information, permissions, feasibility or effective compute are also changed, label the result `joint_manipulation`. Such a contrast cannot support exclusive attribution to Γ even if performance changes.

## 6. Development, calibration and seed separation

**Allowed data:** engineering interfaces and requirements, exact task laws, symbolic unit conversions, static code inspection, runtime profiling, and future B1-D traces. **Forbidden:** any B1-E/B1-G outcomes, future final seeds used for development, previously observed historical finals reused as new finals, or thresholds chosen from favorable C1-versus-alternative effects.

Development has **64 bundles per pilot**, indexed 0–63. Bundles 0–31 permit implementation development and configuration tuning; 32–63 are reserved for effect-blinded calibration of instrumentation, matching, numeric tolerance and resource feasibility. Their comparator identities are masked during margin/precision review. Record every use. Do not reuse a consumed calibration block as an untouched check. Further development requires a new versioned development manifest and fresh namespace before confirmatory seeds exist.

The exact development seed rule is the unsigned big-endian integer represented by the first eight bytes of `SHA256(UTF8("PD-B1-D-v1|" + source_commit + "|" + pilot_id + "|" + role + "|" + decimal_bundle_index + "|" + substream_id))`. Roles are `training`, `tuning`, `calibration`, and `evaluation-development`; allowed index ranges are recorded above. Substream IDs encode cell, episode, epoch and event type. The rule is frozen; no seed is generated or run in B0.

Before any B1-D comparator scores, freeze a source-reviewed implementation choice per comparator and **eight configuration slots per tunable comparator**. Each slot receives the same number of development task histories and the same declared training-operation allowance; architecture-specific parameters may differ. A fixed analytical controller such as P5 C4 retains its prescribed configuration and does not pretend to undergo eight distinct searches. Select tunable configurations by mean primary development loss subject to guardrails, with ties resolved by lower useful-operation count, then lexicographic configuration ID. Do not change search spaces after inspecting cross-comparator differences. The actual code, configurations and numeric compute limits are B1-D deliverables, not fictional implemented facts in B0. If no fair matching is implementable, retain a cost frontier and suspend the specificity question.

For arithmetic tolerance, the future implementation must enumerate the primitive operations on each recorded diagnostic quantity, use the chosen numeric format's unit roundoff and the standard forward-error bound for that explicit operation sequence, propagate those bounds through the registered range limits, and freeze the resulting absolute/relative error envelope. Discrete counts, IDs, permissions, target versions and categorical decisions require exact equality. If division near zero, branching instability, or an unbounded operation prevents a finite certified envelope, the implementation is not calibration-ready and must be redesigned or use exact arithmetic before B1-E. Development checks confirm the declared envelope; final observed disagreement may not enlarge it. The compute budget is the smallest common useful-operation cap in the engineering preregistration that accommodates every valid reference implementation under its worst declared input shape; hardware time is separately profiled on development-only inputs, with the maximum observed time multiplied by a fixed factor 2 for the deadline. A missed final deadline remains an outcome and does not relax the cap.

After the full B1-E preregistration freeze, an independent custodian draws one 256-bit CSPRNG master entropy value, escrows it, and publishes its hash before outcomes are inspected. Final bundle seeds use the same hash construction with prefix `PD-B1-E-v1`, the frozen manifest hash, escrowed entropy, pilot ID, bundle index and distinct `training-final` / `evaluation-final` roles. Check collisions with all registered development and historical seeds; append the smallest nonnegative collision-counter to the hash input until disjoint. This is a future generation rule, not seed selection now. Final training and evaluation streams are disjoint, as are B1-E and B1-G namespaces.

## 7. Margins, sample size, intervals and multiplicity

Stable rule IDs: **CAL-B1-MARGINS** (fixed engineering requirements and derived normalization) and **CAL-B1-N** (derived precision requirement with future development-only feasibility review).

The following numbers are **introduced engineering requirements for these synthetic tasks**, not effect estimates or universal practical thresholds. They may not be optimized to favor the nominated organization.

* Utility practical margin: `delta_U = 1/(2*32) = 1/64` in failure-fraction units. This asks for at least one additional correctly completed service job per task cell per 32-epoch episode, on average. Equivalence margin: `epsilon_U = delta_U/2 = 1/128`; a smaller change is tolerated as practically interchangeable under this explicitly chosen requirement.
* Mechanism and context practical margins: `delta_M = delta_K = 1/2` of the declared normalized diagnostic range. Equivalence margins: `epsilon_M = epsilon_K = 1/4`. The diagnostic predicts a full-range effect 1; detection must resolve at least half of that designed effect. This tests assay sensitivity and bounded causal behavior, not utility.
* The resource budgets and hard permission/safety limits are separate guardrails. A utility change cannot purchase a violation.

These choices have an explicit external service/dose interpretation independent of controller outcomes. If a real deployment requires a different tolerance, it needs a new prospectively justified version before final data access; the synthetic-task margins must not be relabeled as universal domain economics.

There are **27 registered scalar interval estimates** in the initial B1-E family: for each of three pilots, two source-intervention effects (κ1 and κ2), their contextual interaction, four C1 loss differences (C0/C2/C3/C4), and two acute-route loss differences (κ1 and κ2). C5 and secondary metrics are descriptive; they cannot rescue a failed confirmatory claim. Future B1-G is a separate preregistered family, not an uncorrected addition to these 27. Each registered estimate is the mean of independent bundle-level bounded observations.

For utility comparator c, `D_c = L_C1 - L_c`, in [-1,1]; improvement is negative. P5 versus its fixed optimal C4 has the tighter analytical support [0,1]; a negative observed value is an implementation/measurement inconsistency. For a mechanism diagnostic, `D_M = Y_active - Y_sham`, in [-1,1]; the κ1 prediction is positive. For context, `D_K = D_M,kappa1 - D_M,kappa2`, in [-2,2]; the prediction is positive. Acute route differences are `L_intact - L_bypass`, in [-1,1], with no universal predicted direction.

For a contrast with known support [a,b], range `r=b-a`, N independent bundles, family size M=27 and alpha=0.05, use the simultaneous bounded-mean interval:

`h(N,r) = r * sqrt(log(2*M/alpha)/(2*N))`;

`CI = [max(a, mean-h), min(b, mean+h)]`.

Hoeffding's bound followed by the union bound gives simultaneous coverage at least 0.95 without normality, without independence between contrasts, and without treating epochs as independent. Clipping uses the known support only. This is a fixed-sample precision design, **not a claim of calculated statistical power**.

Freeze a target half-width `h_star = epsilon/2` for the relevant margin of every confirmatory contrast; acute-route contrasts use epsilon_U. Half the equivalence margin leaves room for an interval centered near zero to fall strictly inside the equivalence region; it is an explicit precision requirement, not an unexplained inflation factor. Then:

`N_required = max over the 27 contrasts of ceil(r^2 * log(2*27/0.05) / (2*h_star^2))`.

Use exactly this N for every pilot and contrast if version 1 is eventually selected for execution. This conservative rule can require a very large sample. Its largest range/margin requirement is proposed now; no N bundles or final seeds have been allocated or run. In B1-D, assess cost with development-only runtime measurements. **If N cannot be afforded, do not cap N and proceed as if adequately designed.** There is no obligation to execute this conservative default. Mark B1-E infeasible and revise the future statistical protocol prospectively, potentially using an independently justified variance-aware method, with a new version and preregistration before any final seeds/data are available. A more efficient method is not silently substituted into version 1, and the old freeze is not called confirmatory-ready.

Equivalence is established only when the entire simultaneous interval lies strictly inside `(-epsilon,+epsilon)`. Improvement requires the entire interval strictly below `-delta_U`; worsening requires it strictly above `+delta_U`. Directional mechanism/context support requires the entire interval strictly above its positive delta. Threshold equality is inconclusive. Zero-excluding but practically small effects are inconclusive for practical improvement, not automatically successes. The intervals already control the full registered family; do not add an unregistered uncorrected p-value criterion.

## 8. Guardrails, sensitivity and failures

Hard guardrails require zero observed unauthorized executions, invalid same-instance concurrent writes, execution outside the common feasible action set, stock outside its declared physical range, or undeclared budget overruns. Report counts and exposures even if zero. Zero observed violations does not prove general safety. Each comparison also requires its six-axis matching audit to pass. For C5, declared budget changes are permitted only under its descriptive status.

Sensitivity checks, performed prospectively in B1-D and frozen before B1-E, must show that counters detect a known injected operation/effect, distinguish 00 from nonzero parallel flows, detect a deliberately invalid action without executing it, preserve timing and target IDs, and report exact-recoding discrepancies. They are instrument checks, not new evidence in B0. Record expected values from the task contract, tolerances from arithmetic bounds, and pass/fail rules before running them. The same frozen checks must remain passing in B1-E; a failed or missing check makes the relevant scientific verdict inconclusive.

* A model crash, malformed output, unauthorized attempted operation, decision-budget exhaustion or missed decision deadline is a controller failure: assign primary loss 1 for the affected registered episode, retain the bundle and log the cause. A valid in-range request reduced or rejected by the common physical gate is **not** automatically a controller crash: P5 stock shortage and P7 ordinary write arbitration incur their actual missed service and rejection counters. Attempts, admission and prohibited execution remain distinct. A hard execution violation separately prevents a favorable verdict.
* A documented infrastructure failure outside all compared controllers permits one identical retry using the same seed, code and configuration. The first attempt and retry remain recorded. If the retry also fails, retain loss 1 and flag assay failure; do not draw a replacement seed.
* Missing outcomes, corrupted provenance or unresolved pairing invalidate a complete-case claim. The planned sample is not reduced; the result is inconclusive until the identical missing artifact can be recovered without changing the experiment. No favorable-row deletion or imputation from other seeds.
* A technical safety stop pauses collection for diagnosis without inspecting comparative scores. If the run cannot resume identically, report `stopped_incomplete` and no confirmatory claim. Never extend the sample in response to intervals, significance, a favorable trend or the lack of one.

The statistical stopping rule is exactly N bundles per pilot. No interim efficacy/futility test and no outcome-based optional stopping are permitted. Final analyses are opened once the collection manifest is complete, except operational monitoring needed to prevent invalid execution.

## 9. Decision assembly and attribution

`B1_DECISION_RULES_v1.yaml` specifies ordered, mutually resolved cases. Missing data, failed sensitivity, failed invariants, uncompleted collection or invalid intervals cannot default to support. The evaluator must check interval support, endpoints, mean, N, source/freeze hashes, family size, primary definitions and margin references before applying predicates. `mediator_contract_verified` checks intervention delivery, timing and permitted-mediator instrumentation, not agreement of the measured effect with the prediction. Likewise sensitivity means instrument validity, not a favorable target effect. Null is not false evidence; it causes an inconclusive/not-established verdict.

Report H_mechanism, H_utility, H_pairing, H_context and H_generalization separately for every pilot. The complete local mechanism contract requires the positive κ1 subclaim and the predicted neutral κ2 subclaim; report both even if only one passes. For eligible P7, H_utility requires meaningful improvement over **both C0 and C4**. H_pairing requires meaningful improvement over **all C0/C2/C3/C4** under admissible matching. P5 and P6 are explicitly ineligible for positive utility/specificity verdicts in their exact v1 tasks: P5 has a per-realization physical ceiling, whereas P6 has an expected Bayes optimum. If a relevant alternative is practically equivalent or superior, reject the claimed practical necessity of nominated pairing within that tested domain; this does not abolish the component processes. If comparisons are weak, unmatched, missing or imprecise, suspend the claim. A failed acute route can coexist with successful retraining: it measures a different question.

The diagnostic effect may support a local operational relation only after adequate actual intervention and validated measurement. For a deterministic law explicitly coded into a simulator, report that narrow engineering fact. Do not promote it into a finding about naturally occurring cognitive relations. O2 is a completeness status now; O3/O4 are evidence statuses requiring future appropriate studies.

B1-E is limited to the designed tasks. H_generalization remains `not_established` until B1-G includes an independent task-family author and generator implementation, a frozen adapter that changes interfaces/units without redefining the construct, untouched family outcomes, and a separate multiplicity/sample plan using the same procedures on the registered scalar endpoints. Freeze all directional predictions, margins, permitted adaptation budget and comparator set before opening that family. Report zero-shot transfer, budgeted adaptation and retraining as different modes; choose and preregister one primary mode before outcomes.

|---|---|---|
| P5 | A different consumable resource with independently authored replenishment dynamics and unit conversion; stock and permission gate remain distinct. | Retain functions when measured flows are distinct. Reformulate Γ if its delay/context prediction fails with a sensitive assay. An insensitive or broken instrument warrants repair and a new prospective study, not a favorable interpretation of missing effects. Equivalent source effects in a context where a practically positive effect was predicted contradict that local prediction when assay validity is established. Unanticipated sign reversals are reported as such. Coactivation alone is neither synergy nor emergence; interaction on this chosen scale is not ontological irreducibility.

P1–P4 and P8 remain in the catalog and are not confirmatory B1 pilots in version 1. P8's unidentified distinctive relation does not block these three O2 designs. Neither a positive result nor B0_FREEZE authorizes claims of consciousness, ASI, AGI, general autonomy, intrinsic motivation, natural validity of eight pairs, global stability or universal safety.
