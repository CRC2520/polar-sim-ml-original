# P4-NEURO — prospective neurobiological correspondence protocol

Status: **unexecuted external-data protocol**. No neurobiological correspondence is currently established.

## Question

Do independently measured biological signals support a mapping to the computational functions used in POLAR without choosing the mapping after observing the result?

## Required preconditions

Before accessing the final biological outcomes, specify:
- biological preparation/population and measurement modality;
- native observable variables and temporal resolution;
- mapping from computational constructs to biological measurements;
- admissible coordinate transformations;
- lesion/intervention analogues;
- positive and negative controls;
- primary statistical endpoints and multiplicity correction.

## Candidate computational constructs to map

The current evidence permits testing, but does not biologically identify:
1. differentiated activation + coactivation/intensity;
2. prediction-error state;
3. contextual relation/routing;
4. source/self-vs-world attribution;
5. higher-order confidence/metacognitive error monitoring;
6. temporal provenance/memory;
7. global content availability/broadcast;
8. action-conditioned counterfactual consequence estimates.

The historical eight semantic pairs are **not** the default biological mapping because P1 did not support a universal fixed catalogue.

## Discriminating intervention logic

A useful biological correspondence must predict selective patterns rather than generic activity loss. At minimum:
- a relational-pathway perturbation should alter selected integration/source/meta endpoints more than matched first-order behavior;
- a broadcast perturbation should disrupt cross-module availability/decision access;
- a higher-order perturbation should preferentially affect confidence/error monitoring while preserving matched first-order performance;
- sham and matched random-site controls must be included.

## Decision boundary

A correspondence claim requires prospective mapping plus external biological data.
Similarity of qualitative diagrams, post-hoc correlation or naming a signal "tension" is insufficient.

This protocol cannot establish phenomenal consciousness by itself; it only tests whether the computational organization has an independently supported biological analogue.
