# P1 — prospective completion campaign, 20 September 2026

## Scope

This campaign completes the **evaluation work** of the recent P1 agenda:
composition/transmission, local-to-population effects, learned ecological cost,
credit alignment under fixed experimental conditions, and internal tension
propagation. Completing an evaluation does not require favorable results and
does not certify that the full research model has closed every scientific gap.

This is a new, explicitly descriptive campaign based on preserved implementations.
It is not a relabeling of historical P1, P5/P6, consciousness gaps 1–6, or the
September 18 confirmatory A/C design. The latter requires its own power analysis,
manipulation validity and preregistered inference; this campaign cannot silently
inherit that status. A parameter discount is not a finite planning horizon.

## Experimental fronts

### A — population composition and copying versus actual private income

Use the eight-composition grid 0.35, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70,
two transmission rules, and base/copy-score equalization/resource pooling.
Retain the engine's 20 generations, 400 steps, six groups and 20 agents per group.
Distinguish the copying score, real income, and private survival gap. Resource
pooling conserves total harvested resources at the intervention step; it may
change subsequent behavior and ecology as a mediated effect. It does not isolate
the contribution of every downstream copying channel.

### C — local regulator to population dynamics

Use the same composition grid and population/learning budgets. Factorial
interventions cross decision use of the vitality critic, its discount, and
directed versus rate-matched blind exploration. Separate learning the critic from
using it. Include matched shared-successor and invertible-coordinate controls.
Architecture factors are never transmitted as learned state. In C, initial
composition is a prior on Q, not a permanent force on action; report measured
restraint separately from ancestral labels.

Population p-star estimates must state whether a transition is actually bracketed
by the sampled grid. Multiple crossings, no crossings, or an endpoint outside the
grid must not be converted into a fabricated numerical threshold. A composition
curve after a bounded run does not by itself demonstrate asymptotic basins or
hysteresis.

### E — learned ecological consequence and credit assignment

The E design specifies repeated experimental anchors, one randomized own action
per observed training trial, fixed continuation, and public observed consequences.
Diagnostic counterfactual evaluations are separated from training experience.
Current observations, targets and physical tapes are preserved for the credit
alignment control; only their trial-assignment key is shifted. This is an
inter-trial credit intervention, not a change in the world's regeneration delay.

Learned consequence magnitude is distinct from a self-created ecological objective:
the value attached to preserving resources is supplied by the experimenter.
Prediction, intervention calibration, and native-policy utility are separate
endpoints. An algebraically equivalent generic model is an implementation control;
a different equal-capacity predictor is a performance comparator.

### T — eight-polarity tension propagation

Use direct state interventions, sham, network-off, W-only/K-only, edge/topology
controls, and a tension-mediator clamp. Equal context, external observations,
future noise and a nonbinding budget isolate internal propagation from shared
resource projection. Distinguish topological response from external task utility.
The matrices W/K are configured hypotheses, not learned semantic relations. The
presence of propagation does not prove emergence, exclusive representation value,
or consciousness. A mathematically invertible coordinate change must preserve the
physical interpretation.

## Inference and release gate

The seed is the inferential unit. Conditions within a front share paired seed
keys; agent-steps are never treated as independent replications. Report raw
per-seed results, uncertainty and failures, including extinctions. Confidence
intervals are descriptive unless a module explicitly defines a prospective
adjustment; they do not create a global confirmatory PASS by counting favorable
tests. Thirty seeds are a fixed bounded budget, not a claim of guaranteed power.

Before final execution: review implementations and meaningful invariants; run only
development seeds; freeze source/configuration/seed lists in a versioned record;
verify preserved source hashes. Afterwards: retain all conditions, recompute
summaries from raw output, perform predeclared replay checks, and report scientific
outcomes separately from engineering pass/fail.

Detailed numerical and storage contracts reside in the module design/manifest
files. No module may alter another front or a preserved engine to rescue a result.
If a scientific source bug is found after opening final data, preserve the run,
mark it invalid for its affected claim, and use a separately named correction
with new seeds rather than overwrite evidence.

The final report must distinguish: executed and supported; executed and adverse;
executed but statistically uncertain; and not identified by the implemented assay.
None is a measure of phenomenal consciousness.
