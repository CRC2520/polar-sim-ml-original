## H1. Scientific targets

Keep H_REL, H_CAT and H_TRANSFER separate: useful functional organization; correspondence with the historical catalogue under a documented native map; and independent-task transfer. H_CAT is currently NOT_EVALUABLE. P5/P6 remain negative controls. P7 cfg01 and its zero paired/route development effects remain archived, without retuning. A new learner cannot be declared to have repaired those outcomes.

The target is the static support S of an explicitly defined decision-time message operator. Do not learn B_hat and relabel its coefficients as organizational links. Do not add fields after planning and describe them as regulatory inputs. A learned edge is a computational route until a suitable intervention establishes its functional contribution; it is not automatically a causal link in the environment.

For the first comparison, support is fixed after discovery. Do not learn support, arbitrary contextual gating, physical dynamics and new observation adapters as one uninterpretable intervention. Theta may be trained within a common declared message family; its budget and interaction with support selection must be controlled across arms. Context-dependent support is deferred.

## H3. Admission before a scientific run

No existing task is automatically admitted. P7 can provide an instrument reference, not a free pass around its selective-intervention limitation. Do not combine separate P5/P6/P7 environments to invent common nodes, reuse the old eight-valve P1 system as sixteen catalogue channels, select a contextual unit, or type actuators by philosophical names.

The next change must provide: source/version/license of the domain; native node-to-actuator map; costs/permissions/decoder; feasible state regimes; raw observation parity; a nontrivial alternative support; and a symmetry argument. Permutations that preserve physical and informational anchors can make nominally different supports indistinguishable. Such an alternative is an identity control, not a structural rival.

Require a selective decision-route intervention that can alter only declared route use while retaining raw information, memory and feasible actions. Keep computing the bypassed message when appropriate for logical-resource matching. A joint source/sham diagnostic may test the instrument but cannot open a Gamma-selective claim.

## H4. Comparator contract

Use the new IDs S-M0 through S-M6 rather than rewriting C0–C6: independent local routes; mapped historical support when evaluable; a typed alternative support; learned sparse support; learned dense support; a competitively trained generic non-graph controller; and a competent conventional controller. A suitable recurrent or RL baseline must be chosen from the task contract, not prescribed by a fashionable algorithm name.

All relevant arms retain raw observations and feasible action repertoire. Audit maximum opportunity and actual use for information, feasibility, learning, memory, planning and decision computation. A higher-capacity comparator is not claimed to be exactly capacity-matched. Do not pad cheap controllers with useless work to make elapsed time equal.

The orientation–intensity recoding and algebraically identical generic formulation are identity checks, not competitors to defeat. Transform restrictions, decoder and complete state consistently.

## H5. Data and audit design

Discovery selects support/regularization only on development data. Freeze selected structure, weights, feature construction and decision rules before held-out causal and utility evaluation. Retain failed searches. Acute lesions and competitive retraining answer different questions and must not share one aggregate verdict.

Predeclare support thresholding, equivalence classes, structural-stability statistic and uncertainty. Different supports with the same intervention behavior may indicate non-identifiability, not absence of useful organization. Catalogue overlap requires a map and a typed degree/density null fixed before discovery outcomes; do not leak catalogue pairing into features or maximize overlap by post-hoc relabeling.

The later confirmatory sample size, margins and namespace are not inherited from B1-E v2. No numerical values or seeds are supplied here. A separate authorization must close protocol, data custody, resources, missing-run treatment and stopping. No optional sample extension after inspecting final outcomes.

## H6. Tests required in the future implementation

Test native channel accessibility and doses; full-state recoding; raw-information parity; directed route use; intact/bypass/sham differences; absence of post-plan-only diagnostic routes; symmetry-aware alternative supports; early detection of non-identifiable comparisons; negative controls; retention of failures; exporter completeness; and disjoint split identities. Static consistency checks in this release are not substitutes for those future scientific instrument tests.

No graph is designated polar merely because it is sparse, stable or useful. H_REL support without H_CAT correspondence is a generic relational result. Correspondence without usefulness is not practical support. Neither validates psychological labels, the complete layer map, consciousness or general intelligence.
