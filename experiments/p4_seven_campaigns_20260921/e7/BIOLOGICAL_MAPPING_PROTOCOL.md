# E7 — Prospective biological correspondence protocol

## Status

Not yet executed as a prospective biological validation.

## Predeclared mapping for a future/held-out dataset

### B1 Latent/local persistence
POLAR predicts that task-relevant content can remain decodable in local/posterior or temporal representations while global-access markers are reduced between access events.

Primary test: content decoding during maintenance in local sensory/temporal signals remains above chance while a predeclared global/PFC access measure is lower than at access/report events.

### B2 Context-dependent workspace promotion
When a cue/query makes latent content globally useful, POLAR predicts an increase in a predeclared long-range access/broadcast marker.

Primary test: report/access trials show greater frontoparietal or fronto-sensory information sharing than matched no-report/maintenance trials while controlling sensory input.

### B3 Meta-specific dissociation
Metacognitive confidence/report quality should depend on higher-order/meta signals beyond first-order sensory decoding.

Primary test: a predeclared higher-order signal predicts confidence conditional on matched first-order decoding; perturbation/lesion evidence is preferred.

### B4 Reactivation-dependent memory updating
For the modern latent-unconscious component, contradictory evidence should cause persistent representational updating primarily when the relevant memory is reactivated.

Primary test requires a biological paradigm with matched reactivation/no-reactivation and contradictory/non-contradictory feedback.

## Dataset constraint

Published Cogitate iEEG and M-EEG results are relevant, but their aggregate findings were already available before this mapping was finalized. They may be used for retrospective compatibility and pipeline development, not claimed as prospective validation. A genuinely held-out subset with analysis isolation or a new dataset/experiment is required for the final E7 claim.
