# Functional indicators and limits of inference about consciousness

This is the executable protocol supplement to the single unpublished research manuscript, not a second manuscript. The scientific target and the computational mechanisms must be separated. None of the following assays is a detector of phenomenal consciousness. A successful controller, a memory record, or a useful action-effect prediction does not by itself establish subjective experience.

## Four distinct targets

1. **Phenomenal consciousness:** whether there is experience for the system. The current project has no validated measurement or ground-truth label for this property. Its status is **not established**, rather than inferred present or absent from a numerical score.
2. **Global access:** whether particular information becomes causally available to several otherwise distinct processes, allowing them to use its content flexibly. This is an architectural/functional target. A shared budget does not establish content access.
3. **Functional self-model:** a representation of the system's own capacities, actions and predicted effects that changes its decisions. The current gain estimator implements a narrow action/effect prediction function. It does not identify an enduring subject or distinguish the source of an effect.
4. **Functional metacognition:** evaluating the reliability of one's own decisions and using that evaluation to regulate behavior. Prediction error, confidence bias, sensitivity and task performance must be evaluated separately. The present uncertainty heuristic is not a probability of success.

Theory-derived indicators provide conditional evidence whose interpretation depends on the theory and its transfer to artificial systems. Butlin and colleagues propose a structured approach based on scientific theories; this does not license relabeling every useful computational mechanism as a consciousness indicator. [Butlin et al., journal article, online 2025 / issue 2026](https://www.cell.com/trends/cognitive-sciences/fulltext/S1364-6613%2825%2900286-4), [author-deposited full text](https://hal.science/hal-05373552/document).

The Cogitate adversarial collaboration tested divergent predictions agreed in advance and found support for some predictions while challenging important commitments of both GNWT and IIT. It illustrates the value of discriminating predictions rather than treating one theory as settled. The present artificial-system protocol is an adaptation of that research discipline, **not a replication of the human experiment**. [Cogitate Consortium, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1).

A numerical proxy may be confidently wrong when feedback provenance is wrong. |
| Recurrent-processing approaches | State recurrence and persistent memory exist, but there is no specified sensory hierarchy with local recurrent perceptual inference. | **Future:** interrupt recurrent processing in an explicitly defined perception module while matching feedforward steps or compute and test the predicted temporal effect. | Distinguish recurrence from extra computation and from readout/report requirements. | Recurrence in a regulator is insufficient to claim the particular mechanism posited by recurrent-processing theories. |
| Integrated-information approaches | No validated IIT cause-effect model or integrated-information computation is implemented. HGI is graph homogeneity; INC is activation/memory similarity. | **Future:** define the actual transition mechanism and causal intervention distribution before any IIT-related analysis. Compare structures with matched task behavior. | Specify system boundary, grain, discretization and intervention semantics; use the exact theoretical quantity if claimed. | HGI, INC, correlation, network density and multi-agent coordination are not substitutes for IIT's quantity or evidence of phenomenal unity. |

This mapping identifies gaps rather than counting satisfied rows. There is no total indicator count, consciousness percentage or pass/fail classifier for experience. Recurrent and integrated-information rows are prospective theory-specific design requirements, not implemented assessments.

## Executed functional audit

The machine-readable definition is `docs/consciousness_protocol.json`. The audit uses ten fixed diagnostic seeds, 73001–73010, the unchanged contextual model, three agents and eight two-channel types. These are **exploratory mechanism probes** devised with knowledge of the existing architecture and earlier results. They are not an external preregistration, an independent confirmatory evaluation, or tests on humans.

### Cue-only memory

Each seed generates one target pattern with a dominant channel per type. Eight identical presentations train the cue association. Four copies receive the same action-state reset and then no observed target: intact memory; erased cue; a record edited to the opposite channel convention; or an unknown cue. The known cue is an address, not a repetition of the hidden target. Paired differences in action MSE relative to the stored target quantify the memory's functional contribution. Full states show whether the intervention actually reached the record. The orientation comparison is computed from output arrays, not from an intervention label.

### Action-effect prediction and source contamination

Every condition sees the same 25 replayed own actions. Replay is imposed through the existing `q` setter and zero planning horizon, an explicit laboratory intervention rather than autonomous behavior. Training supplies either true own effects with channel gains sampled from [0.55, 0.75], or effects with gains `2 - own_gain`. A third copy of the correctly trained model has its entire capability state reset: gains, counts and prediction-error estimates. This contrast tests the learned capability state jointly, not a gain-only lesion. At test, a fixed action of 0.25 in every channel isolates prediction differences, and a separate decision from zero action state tests functional use of the estimate. The evaluator always uses the true own gain at test.

The contamination deliberately breaks the action/effect pairing assumption. The current API accepts a supplied effect without inferring its actor. Therefore successful native prediction and vulnerability to contaminated feedback can coexist. This is a boundary of the implemented function, not an assertion that every self-model must solve arbitrary mislabeled data. The raw uncertainty values are archived; they must not be interpreted as calibrated credibility about source identity.

### Allocation and content identity

Two arrays contain different channel contents but equal aggregate demand per agent. Under uniform weights/costs and an identical budget, their allocations are compared. Allocation invariance characterizes aggregation by demand. It is not a positive global-access test; no information transfer to a new consumer is measured.

All inputs, interventions, state snapshots, actions, effects and prediction outputs are retained in `results_study2/functional/traces.json.gz`. `manifest.json` contains runtime versions and source/artifact SHA-256 values. Tables are computed from those traces. Descriptive 95% bootstrap intervals resample the ten paired seeds 2,000 times; they describe this synthetic generator only. Channels and time steps are not counted as independent samples. No significance claim, multiple-comparison decision rule or optimized success threshold is used.

```bash
python scripts/run_functional_probes.py
python scripts/run_functional_probes.py --regenerate
python -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_functional_probes.py' -v
```

The generated `REPORT.md`, `summary.json` and `functional_results.tex` are the result records. The manuscript incorporates their conclusions into its single current draft. Tests validate intervention delivery, matched action history, trace regeneration, and the fact that these assays are not a consciousness classifier.

## Prospective independent study: not executed

The following work requires functions or data that do not yet exist in this repository. Naming it here does not report it as completed.

1. **Specify competing claims before adding architecture.** For global access, require causal content transfer to multiple independent consumers. For metacognition, require a forecast about the system's own future decision quality that changes its choice to act, abstain or seek information. For self/other attribution, require competing source hypotheses and interventions that distinguish actions by different actors. State what outcome would weaken each claim and what a nonconscious controller could also achieve.
2. **Construct adequate measures.** Emit a probability of trial success before outcome feedback, under a fixed primary task. Report Brier score and calibration curve, type-2 discrimination, and primary-task accuracy separately; assess metacognitive efficiency only under explicit signal-detection assumptions and sufficient trials. Fit any probability calibration on development data only. Do not convert the current count/error heuristic to a probability by renaming or arbitrary rescaling. [Fleming and Lau, 2014](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2014.00443/full).
3. **Freeze predictions and obtain independent evaluation.** Have reviewers representing at least two theories and a theory-neutral evaluator agree on the mechanism mapping, nuisance controls and divergent predictions. Freeze code, measures, exclusions, smallest effect of interest and sample-size analysis before allocating an untouched test set. Conceal controller identities from the evaluator until analysis is locked. No independent human panel has been recruited and no external registration has been submitted in this work.
4. **Compare matched explanations.** Include a conventional memory/predictive controller, a feedforward or message-disconnected version where appropriate, selective lesions with sham interventions, and an equal-compute control. Prevent hidden-target, source-label and answer-key leakage. Evaluate successful and failed task regimes rather than choosing only helpful perturbations.
5. **Separate computational and biological evidence.** Any use of neural datasets must define in advance what model quantity corresponds to each neural measure, include appropriate report/task controls, and acknowledge limits of extrapolation between substrates. The Cogitate study is a methodological reference, not a source of consciousness labels for these agents. No neural-data analysis or human study is reported here.
6. **Publish conclusions by construct.** A supported functional prediction licenses a claim about that function and tested conditions. Failure narrows or rejects the stated functional hypothesis. Neither outcome alone settles phenomenal consciousness. Claims about life, the universe or polar metaphysics require separate operational predictions and independent evidence and are not inferred from controller performance.

The immediate executable next step in this stream is a source-aware action/effect interface plus a calibrated decision-quality monitor, tested against equally informed conventional estimators. A global content-access architecture should be developed only with a task requiring such access and controls that separate content transfer from resource allocation. These are prospective additions, not retroactive descriptions of the current model.
